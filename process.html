Process

A deliberate path from idea to production, with written scope, working software early and evidence before anything goes live.

01

Map.

Find where AI pays off, then specify it.

We interview the people who do the work, map the workflows and data, score every opportunity by value, effort and risk, and write the specification for the first build: its job, tools, permissions, approvals and success criteria.

What happens

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Workflow and data mapping
  • Scored opportunity map
  • Agent specification and approval model

What you get

  • A clear first build
  • Target architecture
  • Success criteria agreed in writing

03

Prove.

Evidence, not confidence.

We build evaluation sets from your real cases, run the system against them and fix what fails. A supervised pilot and a release gate decide when it is ready, then we launch in stages with a rollback path.

What happens

  • Golden evaluation sets
  • Regression runs
  • Supervised pilot
  • Staged rollout

What you get

  • Release gate report
  • Known limitations, documented
  • Go-live decision with you

04

Run.

Improve every month.

With managed operations we monitor quality and cost, re-run evaluations, tune prompts and policies, maintain connectors and add new use cases from a shared backlog.

What happens

  • Monitoring and incident response
  • Monthly evaluation and tuning
  • Connector maintenance
  • New use cases

What you get

  • Monthly performance review
  • Change log and release gates
  • Roadmap updates
