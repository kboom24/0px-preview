From signal to outcome.

01

A request arrives.

An email, a call, a form, a webhook or a schedule. Every agent starts with a clear signal.

02

The agent reasons.

It reads the context, checks your policies and plans the steps, using the model best suited to the job.

03

It acts in your systems.

Scoped, permissioned access to your CRM, help desk, database and APIs. Every call is logged.

04

You stay in command.

Anything irreversible or high-stakes waits for a person. Approve, edit or stop in one tap.

05

Done. And measured.

The result is written back, the customer is updated, and every step is traced and evaluated.
