Questions.

How long does a first agent take?

It depends on the workflow, the systems involved and the review your team needs. Every engagement starts with a written scope and timeline agreed before work begins.

Which models do you use?

We are model-agnostic. We choose models per task for quality, speed, cost and data requirements, and can route between providers with fallbacks.

Can an agent take actions without a person?

Only the actions you allow. We define permissions per tool and put human approval in front of anything irreversible or high-stakes.

Where does our data go?

Data handling is agreed per engagement, including which providers process it, retention and access. We can deploy inside your cloud where required.

What happens after launch?

You own the system and its documentation. Many clients choose managed operations so we monitor, evaluate and improve the agents every month.
