Approach

Precision is
the product.

0px is an AI agent and automation studio. We design, build and operate the intelligence layer of modern businesses.

In design, 0px means nothing in the way. That is our standard for AI. No gap between what you *intend and what *happens.

Six principles.

How we decide what to build, and how we build it.

Outcomes over demos

We measure success by work completed in production, not by how impressive a prototype looks.

Humans in command

People approve what matters. Agents earn autonomy one permission at a time.

Measured, not assumed

Every agent ships with evaluations and tracing. If we cannot measure it, we do not ship it.

Your data, your rules

Data handling, providers and retention are agreed up front. We can deploy inside your cloud.

Model-agnostic

We pick the right model for each task and design for the next one, so you are never locked in.

You own it

Code, prompts, evaluations and documentation are yours. We make your team capable of running it.
