Questions.

Which platform do you build on?

Whatever fits your stack and team: n8n, Make, Zapier or custom code. We recommend per workflow and explain the trade-offs.

What happens when something breaks?

Every automation has retries, alerts and a named owner. Failures surface in a channel your team watches instead of failing silently.

Do we own the automations?

Yes. They run in your accounts where possible, with documentation and a handover.

Can AI steps be part of a workflow?

Yes, for classification, extraction and drafting. Anything consequential can pass through a human approval step.
