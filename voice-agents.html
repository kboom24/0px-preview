Questions.

Will callers know they are speaking to AI?

Disclosure wording is agreed with you and your counsel. We recommend clear disclosure at the start of every call.

Can it transfer to a person?

Yes. Warm transfer passes the caller and a summary of the call to your team, with fallbacks if nobody is available.

Which languages are supported?

Language support depends on the voice stack chosen and is confirmed per project.

What about outbound calls?

Outbound calling is subject to consent and calling rules. We only design outbound flows with consent records in place and your counsel's review.
