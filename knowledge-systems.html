Questions.

Does it respect who can see what?

Yes. Access rules from your source systems are carried into retrieval so people only get answers from content they can already see.

How do you reduce wrong answers?

Grounding in retrieved sources, citations, confidence thresholds with handoff, and evaluation on your real questions before launch.

How does it stay up to date?

Sources are re-indexed on a schedule or on change, and outdated content can be flagged by users.

Where is the index stored?

Hosting is agreed per engagement, including inside your own cloud where required.
